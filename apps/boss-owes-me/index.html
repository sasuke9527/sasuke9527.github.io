Boss Owes Me: see what unpaid overtime really costs

What unpaid overtime costs

Boss Owes Me is an iPhone app that turns your overtime into a simple number: how many minutes of every hour you work go unpaid, and how your nominal hourly wage compares with your effective one. Overtime starts when you arrive at work and stops when you leave, and several sessions a day are handled cleanly. There is no account, your wage, location and photos stay on your phone, and it's a paid download with no in-app purchases or subscription.

Key features

  • Real hourly wage: overtime shown as unpaid minutes per hour worked, with nominal and effective hourly wages side by side
  • Automatic tracking: once turned on, overtime starts when you arrive at the workplace you set and stops when you leave; a Siri shortcut and a Lock Screen widget work too
  • Several overtime sessions a day, with overlapping periods merged
  • Optional photo or location check-ins, with time-and-place watermarked photos kept inside the app
  • Shareable images and a yearly summary poster; share images never include your workplace location or check-in photos

Who it's for

  • Results are for personal reference only and are not legal or financial advice

FAQ

How does automatic tracking work?

Set your workplace in the app and turn on automatic tracking. iOS tells the app when you enter or leave that area, and the app records those two moments. You can also start and stop by hand with Siri or the Lock Screen widget.

Is my location uploaded?

No. The app records only when you enter and leave the workplace area, never a movement trail, and location data never leaves your phone. You can turn off location access in iOS Settings at any time and everything else keeps working.

Can I rely on the results?

The calculations are for personal reference only and are not legal or financial advice.
