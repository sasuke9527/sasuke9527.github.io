FossilPets Pixel: hatch a fossil egg, raise an ancient creature

Raise 226 ancient creatures

FossilPets Pixel (Fossil Pets on your Home Screen) is a retro pixel virtual pet game for iPhone. Dig up a fossil egg, hatch it, then feed, play, clean, heal and tuck it in — how you care for it decides which ancient creature it finally becomes. There are 45 evolution lines and 226 forms, all original paleo-inspired pixel designs. It plays offline with no account and no ads, and the full version is a one-time unlock with no subscription.

Key features

  • Start from a fossil egg: dig it up, hatch it, then feed, play, clean, heal and put it to sleep
  • Care decides evolution: few care mistakes and a regular rhythm lead to branch A, otherwise branch B — both are full forms
  • 45 evolution lines and 226 forms, all original paleo-inspired pixel designs; raise an Ultimate in every other class to unlock the mythical class
  • 15 habitat classes, each with its own colour pixel scene and weather
  • Evolution conditions are always visible, and the Replay Hall lets you rewatch every evolution
  • Raise several pets at once
  • Meters drop slowly in real time, so checking in once or twice a day is enough; offline decay is capped at 12 hours

Free and paid

The free version has 4 pet slots and random egg digging. A one-time Full Unlock removes the slot limit, lets you choose which evolution line to dig, and reveals the whole evolution tree in the dex. No subscription, no ads and no consumables; Family Sharing is supported.

Who it's for

Fans of retro virtual pets, pixel art and dinosaurs and other prehistoric life, and anyone who wants an offline, ad-free pet game that takes a few minutes a day.

What it doesn't do

  • No network connection and no account
  • Saves stay on the phone; iCloud sync isn't available yet
  • Not available on the mainland China App Store

FAQ

How much time does it take each day?

Meters drop slowly in real time, so checking in once or twice a day is enough. Offline time is capped at 12 hours of decay, so a few days away won't wreck your pet.

What's the difference between free and the full version?

Free has 4 slots and random digging. The one-time Full Unlock gives unlimited slots, lets you choose which evolution line to dig, and reveals the full evolution tree.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

Neither. The game runs fully offline with no account.

Are there ads or subscriptions?

No ads, no subscriptions and no consumables; the full version is a single purchase.

Is it available in mainland China?

Not at the moment. In other regions, check the App Store for availability.
