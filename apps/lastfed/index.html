LastFed: baby feeding, nappy and sleep tracker

Breastfeeding, diaper & sleep

LastFed is an iPhone app for new parents that puts the 3am answers on the first screen: how long since the last feed, roughly when the next one is due, and which side to start on. Feeds, nappies and sleep take one tap each. There is no account, logging is free forever, and Pro is a one-time purchase rather than a subscription.

Key features

  • Left and right breastfeeding timer that keeps running when you close the screen or switch apps, visible on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
  • Bottle feeds in 5 ml steps with your usual amounts one tap away, plus a reference amount based on official guidance and your baby's weight
  • One-tap nappies (wee, poo or both) and sleep (asleep, awake); a quick-log sheet keeps feeds, nappies, sleep and spit-ups together
  • Night mode with dim amber colours, big buttons and no sounds
  • Feed intervals worked out for you, with a reminder for the next feed at the interval you choose, and a 24-hour rhythm chart for the week
  • Nappy, skin and jaundice photos filed by the time they were taken and kept out of your Photos library, ready for a doctor's visit
  • Growth charts with WHO percentiles (the same ones as the NSW Blue Book), and a guide based on Australian government and public health sources: choking first aid, infant CPR, when to see a doctor, safe sleep, feeding amounts, milestones and vaccine schedules (Australia / mainland China)
  • For the whole family: phones signed in to the same iCloud account share one set of records, and Family Album shares photos with relatives on their own Apple Accounts

Free and paid

Free forever: feed, nappy and sleep logging, the breastfeeding timer with Lock Screen Live Activity, feed reminders, the last 7 days of stats, first-aid and when-to-see-a-doctor guides, vaccine reminders and iCloud sync. A one-time Pro purchase adds today's care (vitamin D, bath, nails and a temperature chart), 14- and 30-day stats, your baby plotted on the growth charts, custom photo tags and Doctor mode, starting a Family Album, CSV export, voice logging in Chinese, Siri and other shortcuts, and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Family Sharing is supported.

Who it's for

Families with a newborn or a baby under one who want to keep track of feeds — especially parents doing night feeds alone who want to log in as few taps as possible, and anyone who wants nappy and skin photos organised by time for a doctor's visit.

What it doesn't do

  • It does not diagnose; the guide does not replace a doctor, midwife or child health nurse
  • No account system, no community and no ads
  • Voice logging currently understands Chinese only
  • iPhone only for now

FAQ

Is LastFed Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase that never renews, and family members in the same Family Sharing group don't need to buy it again. On a new phone, use Settings → Restore Purchases.

How do two parents share records without an account?

Sign both iPhones in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive turned on, and records sync through your own iCloud. Relatives on their own Apple Accounts can see photos through Family Album (starting one needs Pro; joining is free).

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone and in your own iCloud. There is no developer server, no advertising and no third-party analytics; the developer cannot see your records or photos.

Which languages does it support?

English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Voice logging currently recognises Chinese only and runs on-device.

Where does the guide come from?

It summarises public information from Australian government and public health organisations, and each page cites its source and review date. It is general information only and doesn't replace medical advice; if your baby is unwell, see a doctor, and in an emergency call your local emergency number.
