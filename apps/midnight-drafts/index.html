Midnight Drafts: write it, seal it, decide tomorrow

Write it. Seal it. Decide tomorrow.

Midnight Drafts is a private letter app for iPhone. That 2am message to your ex, your boss or your parents goes here first: write it as a letter, seal it with wax, and open it after a cooling-off period to see whether you still want to send it — then burn it, reseal it, add a note, or send it. Letters stay on your phone behind the app's own passcode. No account; it's a paid download with no in-app purchases or subscription.

Key features

  • Write it down: the thing you want to say but shouldn't say yet becomes a letter
  • Seal it with wax for a cooling-off period you choose, at least one hour; a local notification tells you when it can be opened, without showing any of the letter
  • Open it and decide: burn it, reseal it, add a note, or send it
  • Its own passcode: a 6-digit app passcode that people who know your phone passcode still can't get past, plus an optional reading password for a single letter that can't be recovered if forgotten
  • Time capsules and mood tracking: leave letters for your future self and look back at how you felt
  • Send to one person as an encrypted file that only opens with the passphrase you set, shared through the iOS share sheet; share images are available too

Free and paid

A paid download: pay once for every feature, with no in-app purchases and no subscription.

Who it's for

Anyone who tends to fire off messages in the heat of the moment, and anyone who wants a private place for words they can't say out loud yet.

What it doesn't do

  • It is not a mental health treatment tool; if you are struggling, please reach out to a professional or a local helpline
  • No community or public sharing, and no account, server, ads or analytics
  • The developer has no way to read your letters

FAQ

How long does a letter stay sealed?

You choose the cooling-off period, with a minimum of one hour. When it's up, a local notification lets you know, and the notification never shows the letter's content.

Can anyone else read my letters?

No. Letters are stored encrypted on your phone behind the app's own passcode, and the developer has no way to read them.

Can I still send the message after opening it?

Yes. After opening a letter you can choose to send it, or use Send to One Person to create an encrypted file that the recipient opens with the passphrase you set.

How much does it cost?

It's a paid download. You pay once, with no in-app purchases or subscription.

Midnight Drafts is not a mental health treatment tool.
