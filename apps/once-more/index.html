Once More: hide the answer, then retry the question

Snap questions, hide answers

Once More is an iPhone app for working through the questions you got wrong. Photograph a question, hide its answer with one tap, and the app reminds you when it's time to try it again; you can also lay several questions out on an A4 page and print them. It has no question bank and no AI solving — every question comes from your own papers and notes — and it works offline with no account, keeping everything on your phone.

Key features

  • Photo import from the camera or your library, with automatic straightening and question and answer boxes you can drag to adjust
  • Hide or reveal each answer with one tap, so you work the question first and check afterwards
  • Review reminders scheduled with FSRS or fixed Ebbinghaus intervals, delivered as local notifications
  • Question pools by subject: all, due for review, mastered and custom
  • Print practice sheets: arrange several questions freely on an A4 page and print with AirPrint or export a PDF, then mark the sheet and feed the results back in
  • Four interface languages, with text recognition following the interface language

Free and paid

The free download lets you capture questions, hide answers and review. Printing and exporting, and long-term review, are part of Pro: a one-time purchase with no subscription, and you can try it free for 30 days first.

Who it's for

Students and the parents helping them, and adults preparing for professional and entrance exams — anyone who needs to keep retrying mistakes until they stick.

What it doesn't do

  • No question bank and no answer solving; no AI photo search
  • No account, works offline, no ads
  • iPhone only for now

FAQ

Does it have a question bank or AI solving?

No. The app provides no question bank or solving service; it organises your own mistakes: capture, hide the answer, review on schedule and print.

When does it remind me to review?

Choose the FSRS algorithm or fixed Ebbinghaus intervals. When a question is due, the app sends a local notification.

Can I print my mistakes as a test paper?

Yes. Arrange several questions on an A4 page and print with AirPrint or export a PDF. Printing and exporting are Pro features.

How much does it cost?

It's a free download. Printing, exporting and long-term review are in Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription that you can try free for 30 days.

Where are my questions and photos stored?

Only on your phone. The app works offline with no account, server or ads, and collects no data.

This app provides no question bank and no solving service.
