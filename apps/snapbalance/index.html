SnapBalance: double-entry bookkeeping with four financial statements

Accounting-Savvy Bookkeeper

SnapBalance (formerly Pailiji; 拍小票变报表 in Chinese) is an iPhone app for people who want real books for their household money without learning accounting jargon. Snap a receipt, or say something like “groceries at Coles yesterday, 85”, and it creates a balanced double-entry journal entry; the four financial statements build themselves. There is no account, your data stays on your phone, and Pro is a one-time purchase.

Key features

  • Receipt scanning: by default, Apple's on-device text recognition reads the merchant, total, date and GST, and photos never leave your phone; fix a field once and the same merchant's layout is remembered next time
  • Voice or text entry: speak or type a sentence and get several dated, categorised entries to review and save; it understands relative dates such as “last Friday” and handles loans, repayments, withdrawals and deposits; speech is transcribed on the iPhone and audio isn't kept
  • Real double-entry: every entry is a balanced journal entry with a plain-language explanation, plus templates for things like mortgage repayments, a home purchase settlement, an education fund and depreciation
  • Four financial statements: profit and loss, balance sheet, changes in equity and cash flow, with consistency checks
  • Spending analysis with period comparisons, trend lines and a calendar view
  • Optional cloud recognition: for higher accuracy you can enter your own API key and switch to a cloud model or Azure

Free and paid

The free version covers manual bookkeeping, all four statements and one ledger with no time limit and no ads, plus 10 receipt scans. Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase that adds unlimited receipt scanning, bank-screenshot batch import, multiple ledgers, comparisons and balance trends, CSV export (transactions and journal) and recurring entries. Pro is tied to your Apple ID and works for Family Sharing members.

Who it's for

People who want to run their household like a set of books and see their assets, liabilities and cash flow; people who don't know accounting but want proper books rather than a list of expenses; and finance professionals who want familiar methods for their own money. Day-to-day entry is just snap → confirm → done, with no debits or credits in sight unless you open a manual journal entry.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't connect to banks and provides no payment, lending, investment or financial advice services; it is a local bookkeeping tool
  • No account and no cloud sync; when you change phones, restore from an encrypted iPhone backup (iCloud or computer)
  • iPhone only for now, in English and Simplified Chinese

FAQ

Can I use it without knowing accounting?

Yes. Everyday entry is snapping a receipt and confirming it, with no accounting terms on screen. Debits and credits only appear on the manual journal screen, where every line has a plain-language explanation and templates help. To see what a full set of books looks like, try Settings → Generate Sample Ledger first.

Are my receipt photos uploaded?

Not by default: scanning uses iOS's built-in text recognition and photos stay on your phone. Only if you switch recognition to a cloud model or Azure and enter your own API key is that receipt image sent, directly from your phone, to the provider you chose; the developer never sees it.

What does the free version include?

Manual bookkeeping, all four statements and one ledger with no time limit and no ads, plus 10 receipt scans. Unlimited scanning, multiple ledgers and CSV export are part of Pro.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Restore the new iPhone from an encrypted iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) and the app's data comes with it. Pro users can also export CSV files from Settings → Export.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID; after reinstalling, use Settings → Restore Purchases. Family Sharing members can use it too.

SnapBalance is a local bookkeeping tool made by an independent developer. It provides no payment, lending, investment or financial advice services.
