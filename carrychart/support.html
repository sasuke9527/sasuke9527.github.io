资料存在哪里？开发者看得到吗？

资料只在你的手机和你自己的 iCloud 里，照片、视频、录音、扫描件还会先在手机上加密。我们没有服务器，看不到你的任何资料。不想放 iCloud 的话，在「更多 → 设置 → iCloud 与加密」里关掉「用 iCloud 同步和备份」，资料就只存在这台手机上。详见隐私政策。

换手机后资料怎么过去？

开着 iCloud 同步：新手机登录同一个 Apple 账号，装好 App 打开，稍等一会儿资料会自动出现。关着同步，或者想自己留一份：在旧手机的「更多 → 设置 → 导出全部资料」导出 zip，新手机上用「从备份恢复」选这个 zip。恢复只会添加缺的内容，已经有的不会改，也不会重复。

不小心删了记录怎么办？

删掉的记录先放在「更多 → 最近删除」，保留 30 天，这期间可以恢复。

化验单上的单位和以前的不一样？

没关系。同一个指标记过几种单位（比如血糖的 mmol/L 和 mg/dL）时，走势图会自动换算成最近一次用的单位再画，换算过的数前面有「≈」，原始数值不会被改动。换算不了的会提示你核对单位。

指标名字后面括号里的英文是什么？

是化验单上常印的英文缩写，比如「葡萄糖（GLU）」，方便你对照化验单核对是不是同一项。

复诊提醒、吃药提醒没响？

到 iPhone 的「设置 → 通知 → 随身病历」里打开通知。复诊提醒在前一天晚上 8 点和当天各提醒一次。

拍药盒认得不准？

拍的时候让药名和「用法用量」那一面对着镜头、光线亮一点。认出来的内容都会先给你核对，不对的直接改再保存。

买了 Pro，换手机或重装后怎么恢复？

打开 App →「更多 → 升级 Pro」→「恢复购买」。Pro 是一次性购买，不会自动续费。

App 会给诊断或治疗建议吗？

不会。随身病历是记录工具，帮你把资料记下来、整理好，方便给医生看。App 里的数值范围和医学说法的出处在「更多 → 设置 → 参考来源」。身体不舒服请及时就医；紧急情况请拨打当地急救电话。

換手機後資料怎麼過去？

開著 iCloud 同步：新手機登入同一個 Apple 帳號，裝好 App 開啟，稍等一會兒資料會自動出現。關著同步，或者想自己留一份：在舊手機的「更多 → 設定 → 匯出全部資料」匯出 zip，新手機上用「從備份恢復」選這個 zip。恢復只會新增缺的內容，已經有的不會改，也不會重複。

不小心刪了記錄怎麼辦？

化驗單上的單位和以前的不一樣？

沒關係。同一個指標記過幾種單位（比如血糖的 mmol/L 和 mg/dL）時，走勢圖會自動換算成最近一次用的單位再畫，換算過的數前面有「≈」，原始數值不會被改動。換算不了的會提示你核對單位。

指標名字後面括號裡的英文是什麼？

是化驗單上常印的英文縮寫，比如「葡萄糖（GLU）」，方便你對照化驗單核對是不是同一項。

複診提醒、吃藥提醒沒響？

CarryChart · Support

Questions or feedback: acct9527@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Where is my data stored? Can the developer see it?

Your records stay on your phone and in your own iCloud, and photos, videos, recordings and scans are encrypted on your phone first. We have no server and cannot see any of your data. If you would rather not use iCloud, turn off "Sync and back up with iCloud" under More → Settings → iCloud and encryption and everything stays on this phone only. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

With iCloud sync on: sign in to the same Apple Account on the new phone, install the app and open it; your records appear after a short while. With sync off, or to keep your own copy: on the old phone use More → Settings → Export all data to make a zip, then on the new phone choose that zip with Restore from backup. Restoring only adds what is missing; nothing you already have is changed or duplicated.

I deleted a record by mistake

Deleted records are kept under More → Recently Deleted for 30 days and can be restored during that time.

The unit on my lab report is different from last time

That's fine. When the same test has been recorded in more than one unit (for example glucose in mmol/L and mg/dL), the trend chart converts everything to the unit of the latest result. Converted values are marked with "≈" and your original numbers are never changed. If a unit cannot be converted, the app asks you to check it.

What is the English text in brackets after a test name?

It is the abbreviation usually printed on lab reports, for example "Glucose (GLU)", so you can check the app against your report.

Appointment or medication reminders don't arrive

Turn on notifications in Settings → Notifications → CarryChart. Appointment reminders arrive at 8 pm the evening before and again on the day.

Scanning a medicine box gets it wrong

Point the camera at the side with the medicine name and the dosage directions, in good light. Everything it reads is shown to you first, so correct anything that is wrong before saving.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Open the app → More → Upgrade to Pro → Restore purchases. Pro is a one-time purchase and never renews.

Does the app diagnose or give treatment advice?

No. CarryChart is a record-keeping tool that helps you keep your records organised and ready to show a doctor. The sources for the reference ranges and medical statements in the app are under More → Settings → Sources. If you are unwell, see a doctor; in an emergency, call your local emergency number.
