Apps by FQ

These are the six iPhone apps made by independent developer FQ. They share a few things: no account to sign up for; no developer server, so your data stays on your phone or in your own iCloud; no ads and no data collection; and pricing that is always one-off — a free download with a one-time upgrade, or a paid download — never a subscription.

What they have in common

  • No account — open the app and start
  • No developer server: your data lives on your phone, and anything that syncs goes through your own iCloud
  • No ads, no third-party analytics or ad SDKs, no data collected
  • Pay once, never a subscription
