Joey Play · Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

In short: Joey Play has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and you don't need an account. Looking up parks needs nothing from you. Only if you choose to contribute (a review, photo, update, correction or vote) is something uploaded, and what you post is public to everyone who uses the app.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location is used on your phone to show you on the map and sort parks by distance. It is not uploaded, except in the cases described under "When you contribute".
  • Searches, filters, language and appearance settings stay on your phone.
  • Saved parks, "want to go", "visited" and your own tags are kept on your phone and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced to your other devices through your own iCloud (key-value storage). Only you can see them; we can't.
  • Translating a review into your language uses Apple's built-in Translation on your phone. The text is not sent to us.

When you contribute

Posting requires being signed in to iCloud on your iPhone. There is no separate account. Apple gives the app an anonymous user ID for your iCloud account; we never see your name, email address or Apple Account.

What you post is stored in the app's iCloud public database (Apple CloudKit), in Apple's data centres, which may be outside Australia. Anyone using Joey Play can see:

  • your random animal nickname (for example "Koala 4821"; you can switch to another random one), and the level and badges you have earned;
  • your reviews (stars, text and tags), park updates, corrections, confirmations, votes and photos, and the month you posted them;
  • whether you were at the park when you confirmed, corrected or voted (worked out on your phone; only yes or no is uploaded);
  • the exact location you send when you tap "I'm here" to pin a park's location.

Photos: before upload, your phone shrinks the photo, pixelates any faces it finds, and saves a new copy without the original file's details (such as when and where it was taken). The original never leaves your phone. New photos are only shown to everyone after other users approve them.

Reports: when you report something, the report counts towards hiding it (without your reason). The reason you choose and any note are stored separately and can only be seen by you and the developer.

Community moderation: before posting, your phone checks text for offensive words and links. Content reported by two different people is hidden for everyone. If three of your posts are hidden, that device can no longer post.

What the developer can see

Like any user, we can see public content. Through Apple's CloudKit dashboard we can also see report reasons and the anonymous user ID attached to each post. We use this only to run the community, for example to handle complaints and remove unlawful or abusive content. We do not sell or share it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data the app downloads

The app downloads updates to park and activity information from our website (sasuke9527.github.io, hosted by GitHub). Maps are provided by Apple. If you choose to get directions, Apple Maps or Google Maps opens with the park's location. These requests contain no personal information, although, as with any internet request, those servers can see your IP address.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single review on the park page, or everything at once with Me → My reviews & contributions → Delete everything I've posted. This removes your reviews, confirmations, updates, photos, reports, corrections, votes and level card from the public database.
  • Saved parks and tags you remove in the app are also removed from your iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes everything on your phone, but not what you posted. Delete your posts first, or email us and we will remove them.

Children

Joey Play is made for parents and carers. Please don't post photos of children or anyone's personal information. If you think someone's personal information has been posted, contact us and we will remove it.

Complaints and removal requests

Email us about illegal, abusive or infringing content, with the park name and what the content is. We act on complaints promptly, and on removal notices from the eSafety Commissioner within 24 hours.

Changes

If our privacy practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.

Joey Play · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

一句话：Joey Play 没有广告、没有追踪、没有统计，也不用注册账号。查公园不需要你提供任何东西。只有你自己选择参与（写评价、传照片、报近况、提修改、投票）时才会上传，而且你发的内容所有用这个 App 的人都看得到。

只在你手机上的

  • 你的位置在手机上用来在地图上显示你、按远近排公园，不会上传。例外见下面「你参与贡献时」。
  • 搜索、筛选、语言和外观设置只在手机上。
  • 收藏、想去、去过和你自己加的标签存在手机上；手机登录了 iCloud 的话，会通过你自己的 iCloud（键值存储）同步到你的其他设备。只有你看得到，我们看不到。
  • 把评价翻译成你的语言，用的是 Apple 系统自带的翻译，在手机上完成，内容不会发给我们。

你参与贡献时

发内容需要 iPhone 登录 iCloud，没有另外的账号。Apple 会给 App 一个对应你 iCloud 账号的匿名用户 ID；我们看不到你的名字、邮箱或 Apple 账号。

你发的内容存在这个 App 的 iCloud 公共数据库（Apple CloudKit）里，放在 Apple 的数据中心，可能在澳大利亚以外。所有用 Joey Play 的人都看得到：

  • 你的随机动物昵称（比如「考拉 4821」，可以换成另一个随机的），以及你得到的等级和徽章；
  • 你的评价（星级、文字和标签）、近况、修改、确认、投票和照片，以及发布的月份；
  • 你确认、提修改或投票时人在不在公园（在手机上算，只上传「在」或「不在」）；
  • 你点「我在这儿」给公园标位置时发出的准确位置。

照片：上传前，手机会先把照片缩小、给认出来的人脸打马赛克，再另存一份不带原文件信息（比如拍摄时间和地点）的新图。原图不会离开你的手机。新照片要其他用户看过、同意后才对所有人公开。

举报：你举报时，举报会计入隐藏（不带理由）。你选的理由和补充说明另外保存，只有你和开发者看得到。

大家来把关：发之前，手机会检查文字里有没有脏话和链接。两个不同的人举报的内容会对所有人隐藏；你有 3 条内容被隐藏后，这台手机就不能再发了。

开发者看得到什么

和所有用户一样，我们看得到公开的内容。通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 后台，我们还看得到举报理由和每条内容带的匿名用户 ID。这些只用来维护社区，比如处理投诉、删除违法或骚扰内容。我们不出售、不分享这些数据，也不拿来做广告。

App 下载的数据

App 会从我们的网站（sasuke9527.github.io，由 GitHub 托管）下载公园和活动信息的更新。地图由 Apple 提供。你选择导航时，会用公园的位置打开 Apple 地图或 Google 地图。这些请求不包含个人信息；和所有网络请求一样，对方服务器能看到你的 IP 地址。

删除数据

  • 在公园页面删一条评价，或者在「我的 → 我的评价和贡献 → 删除我发过的全部内容」一次全删：会从公共数据库删掉你的评价、确认、近况、照片、举报、修改、投票和等级卡。
  • 在 App 里取消的收藏和标签，你 iCloud 里的也会一起删掉。
  • 删除 App 会删掉手机上的全部数据，但不会删掉你发过的内容。请先删内容，或者发邮件给我们代删。

儿童

Joey Play 是给家长和看护人用的。请不要发孩子的照片或任何人的个人信息。如果你认为有人的个人信息被发了出来，请联系我们，我们会删除。

投诉和删除请求

违法、骚扰或侵权的内容，请发邮件告诉我们公园名字和是哪条内容。我们会尽快处理投诉；澳大利亚网络安全专员（eSafety Commissioner）的删除通知，我们在 24 小时内处理。

变更

隐私做法有变化时，我们会更新本页并修改生效日期。

Joey Play · Política de privacidad

Vigente desde el 20 de noviembre de 2026

Sin anuncios, sin seguimiento, sin analíticas y sin necesidad de cuenta. Consultar parques no envía nada. Tu ubicación se usa en el teléfono para mostrarte en el mapa y ordenar los parques por distancia, y no se sube (salvo cuando tocas «Estoy aquí» para marcar la ubicación de un parque: entonces se publica tu ubicación de ese momento). Tus parques guardados y etiquetas están solo en tu teléfono y en tu propio iCloud; el desarrollador no puede verlos.

Para publicar reseñas, fotos, novedades, correcciones o votos hay que tener la sesión de iCloud iniciada. Lo que publicas se guarda en la base de datos pública de Apple CloudKit y lo ve cualquier usuario, junto a un apodo de animal aleatorio (por ejemplo, Koala 4821). Las fotos se reducen en el teléfono antes de subirlas, las caras se pixelan y no incluyen la fecha ni el lugar de la toma. Puedes borrar todo lo que publicaste en Yo → Mis reseñas y aportes → Borrar todo lo que he publicado. Contacto: acct9527@gmail.com
