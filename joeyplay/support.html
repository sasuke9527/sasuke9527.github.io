Joey Play · Support

Questions, feedback or complaints: acct9527@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Report illegal or abusive content

In the app, tap Report on the review, photo or update. Anything reported by two different people is hidden for everyone straight away. To ask us to remove something (illegal content, personal information, copyright), email us with the park name and which post it is. We act on complaints promptly, and on removal notices from the eSafety Commissioner within 24 hours.

Where does the park information come from?

From council and NSW Government websites and OpenStreetMap. Each park page lists its sources, and we only include what those pages actually say. Things change, so please check signs on site. If something is wrong, tap Something wrong? Tell everyone on the park page so other parents know.

A park, library or pool is missing

Email us the name and suburb and we'll add it in a data update.

Why can't I post?

  • Posting needs your iPhone to be signed in to iCloud (Settings → your name). There is no separate account.
  • The first time, you'll be asked to agree to the community rules.
  • If three of your posts have been hidden by reports, that phone can no longer post.

My photo isn't showing

New photos are shown to everyone once two other users have checked and approved them. Faces are pixelated automatically before upload, but please photograph the facilities, not people. You can help by checking new photos under Me → Help check new photos.

How do I block someone?

When you report a post, choose Also block this person. You'll no longer see their reviews, photos or updates. Undo it in Me → My reviews & contributions.

How do I delete what I've posted?

Me → My reviews & contributions → Delete everything I've posted. Do this before deleting the app; deleting the app alone doesn't remove your posts.

My saved parks don't sync to my other devices

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices. The iCloud sync section in the Me tab shows the current status.

Why does the app ask for my location?

To show you on the map and sort parks by distance, on your phone. The app works without it: the map starts with all of Sydney. Your location is only shared if you tap I'm here to pin a park's location. See the privacy policy.

Joey Play · 帮助

有问题、建议或投诉，请发邮件到 acct9527@gmail.com，一般 2 个工作日内回复。

举报违法或骚扰内容

在 App 里点评价、照片或近况上的「举报」。两个不同的人举报的内容会马上对所有人隐藏。要我们删除某条内容（违法内容、个人信息、侵权），请发邮件写明公园名字和是哪一条。我们会尽快处理投诉；澳大利亚网络安全专员（eSafety Commissioner）的删除通知，我们在 24 小时内处理。

公园信息是哪来的？

来自议会和新州政府的官网，以及 OpenStreetMap。每个公园页面都列了来源，只收这些页面上写了的内容。情况会变，到现场请以告示为准。发现不对，在公园页面点「信息不对？告诉大家」，让其他家长也知道。

少了某个公园、图书馆或泳池

把名字和郊区发邮件给我们，下次更新数据时加上。

为什么发不了？

  • 发内容需要 iPhone 登录 iCloud（设置 → 你的名字），没有另外的账号。
  • 第一次发会请你同意社区规则。
  • 你有 3 条内容被举报隐藏后，这台手机就不能再发了。

我传的照片看不到

新照片要两位其他用户看过、同意后才对所有人公开。上传前会自动给人脸打马赛克，不过请尽量只拍设施、不拍人。你也可以在「我的 → 帮大家看看新照片」里帮忙看。

怎么屏蔽某个人？

举报时勾选「同时屏蔽这个人」，之后就看不到他的评价、照片和近况了。在「我的 → 我的评价和贡献」里可以解除。

怎么删掉我发过的内容？

「我的 → 我的评价和贡献 → 删除我发过的全部内容」。请在删除 App 之前做；只删 App 不会删掉你发过的内容。

收藏没同步到我的其他设备

两台设备要登录同一个 Apple 账号的 iCloud。「我的」里的 iCloud 同步一栏会显示现在的状态。

为什么要用我的位置？

在手机上用来在地图上显示你、按远近排公园。不给位置也能用，地图会显示整个悉尼。只有你点「我在这儿」给公园标位置时，才会公开你当时的位置。详见隐私政策。
